## Journal data attribution

The bundled journal database (`extension/data/journals.json`, 21,772 journals)
is derived from public datasets and is provided for personal academic use
only. The project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of
the data publishers.

| Dataset | Edition | Publisher |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| 新锐期刊分区表 (Xinrui Journal Partition Table) | 2026 | 新锐学术 (Xinrui Academic) |
| Journal Citation Reports (JCR) impact factors & quartiles | 2025 | Clarivate |
| CCF 推荐国际学术会议和期刊目录 | 2026 | China Computer Federation |
| 国际期刊预警名单 (Early Warning Journal List) | 2025 (final edition) | 中国科学院文献情报中心 |

Upstream aggregation source: [ShowJCR](https://github.com/hitfyd/ShowJCR).
Exact provenance fingerprints (revision, archive/database SHA-256) are
recorded in `journals.json.provenance`.

The 中科院分区表 (CAS Journal Partition Table) was discontinued by its
publisher in 2026; the 2025 edition was its final release. ScholarZone
therefore uses the Xinrui 2026 partition as its primary ranking standard and
does not display CAS partitions.

Redistribution and commercial use of the underlying journal data must comply
with the respective publishers' terms. If you are a rights holder and believe
any content infringes your rights, contact us and it will be removed
promptly.
